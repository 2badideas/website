Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

2Bad Ideas LLC, based in Washington, United States, operates 2badideas.com. This notice covers our website and its browser games. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@2badideas.com.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we use analytics to understand website visits, referral sources, and game starts and improve our website and games. We currently use Umami Cloud to process this information on our behalf. Analytics stay off until you select “Accept”. You can reject analytics and still use the site and games, or change your choice at any time through “Analytics choices.” Withdrawing permission stops future collection but does not delete information already collected.

Analytics include pages visited, referral sources, game-start activity, and basic browser and device information. Our analytics provider processes IP addresses and other technical request information to operate the service, estimate location, and distinguish visits. This is not a guarantee of anonymity. We limit page and referral details to reduce the collection of personal information. Game saves, scores, and individual gameplay inputs are not sent as analytics.

We do not use advertising tracking or session recordings, sell personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If your browser exposes an enabled Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, optional analytics stay off even if you previously accepted.

Cookies and information on your device

We do not use cookies. We use other browser storage to remember your analytics choice and support game preferences, saved progress, and offline play. Your analytics choice is remembered for up to 180 days before we ask again; browser settings may shorten this period.

Browser-game saves and preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us or synced between devices. Into the Deep and Sap & Survive can retain progress and preferences between visits. Sticks in the Woods saves XP, best wave cleared, unlocked abilities, and selected loadout between visits. Closing or reloading starts a new run. Some games also store game files for offline play.

You can remove stored choices, saves, and offline files by closing the games and clearing this site's data in your browser. This can remove progress for all games on this site. Offline game activity is not queued for analytics or sent later.

Hosting, email, and service providers

Our hosting and delivery providers process technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser information, and request times, to deliver and protect the site. Our current host is GitHub Pages; see GitHub's data-collection notice.

If you contact us, we receive your contact details, message, and information you choose to provide. We use this to respond and handle your request. Our service providers process information to provide hosting, analytics, and email services. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address security incidents or protect legal rights.

Legal bases, retention, and international processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on consent for optional analytics, legitimate interests to operate a secure website and respond to correspondence, and legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw consent through “Analytics choices” or object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us.

Analytics are retained for up to six months, unless deleted earlier. Service providers retain separate operational and security logs under their own retention practices. We keep correspondence as long as needed to handle requests and meet legal obligations. Device-local information remains until replaced, expired, or cleared.

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Umami processes analytics under its data processing agreement, including its international-transfer safeguards. Contact us for information about applicable safeguards or to request a copy.

Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your information, or object to its use, by emailing privacy@2badideas.com. We may need information to verify and locate your request. Because we do not attach names or contact details to analytics, we may be unable to connect a particular record to you.

People in the EEA or UK may also complain to their local data protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We do not discriminate against people for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Children, external services, and updates

This is a general-audience site, not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not request children's names, contact details, or registrations through the site or games. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

This notice covers this website and its browser games. The downloadable Stick Together game and external services, including Steam and Discord, may have separate privacy notices.

We will update this notice and its date when our privacy practices change. Changes to the information we collect, how we use or share it, retention, or your choices will be reflected here.
