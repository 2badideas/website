Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

2Bad Ideas LLC, based in Washington, United States, operates 2badideas.com. This notice covers our website and its browser games. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@2badideas.com.

No visitor tracking

We do not use visitor-analytics scripts, tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or session recordings. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The website's fonts are served locally.

Sticks in the Woods runs in your browser. Its controls, scores, and progress stay in memory and are not sent to us. It has no accounts, chat, uploads, or persistent saved progress. It caches local game files for offline play after a successful online visit. Closing or reloading the game starts a new run. You can remove cached files by clearing this site’s browser data.

Into the Deep also runs in your browser. It saves game progress in local storage on your device and caches local game files, including fonts, for offline play. Saved progress is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Hosting and email

GitHub Pages hosts our site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Hosting and delivery services also process technical request information, such as requested pages, browser information, and request times, to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub's data-collection notice.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and handle requests. Our hosting, delivery, and email providers process information to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address security incidents or protect legal rights.

Purposes, location, and retention

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests to operate a secure website and respond to correspondence, and on legal obligations where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Contact us for information about applicable international-transfer safeguards. We keep correspondence as long as needed to handle it and meet legal obligations. Providers retain operational and security logs under their own retention practices.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your information, or object to its use, by emailing our privacy address. We may need information to verify and locate your request. People in the EEA or UK may also complain to their local data protection authority. We do not discriminate against people for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change our site's behavior: we do not run visitor-tracking scripts or sell or share information for advertising.

Children, external services, and updates

This is a general-audience site, not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not request children's names, contact details, or registrations through the site or game. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

This notice does not cover the downloadable Stick Together game, purchases on Steam, or use of Discord. Those services may have separate privacy notices. Steam and Discord links take you to services governed by their operators' privacy policies.

We will update this notice and the date above when our practices change, including when new browser games collect different information.
