Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

2Bad Ideas LLC, based in Washington, United States, operates 2badideas.com. This notice covers our website and its browser games. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@2badideas.com.

Optional website analytics

If you select “Accept analytics,” we load Umami Cloud to measure page visits, referral sources, and game starts, including new runs and loaded games. Until you accept, we do not load its script or send analytics requests. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the site or playing the games. Open “Analytics choices” on any page to accept or withdraw permission. Withdrawal stops future collection; it does not delete information already sent.

Umami receives the page path and title, referring website origin, event time, browser language, screen dimensions, and the game name for a game-start event. We remove query strings and URL fragments from analytics payloads and send only the origin of referring websites. Umami also processes technical request information, including IP address and browser information, to deliver requests, derive approximate location, and calculate rotating visitor identifiers. This allows approximate visitor and repeat-visit counts within a rotation period. These identifiers do not guarantee anonymity or reliably link visits across periods or devices. Umami states that IP addresses are not stored in standard analytics records, but they may appear in separate operational or security logs. See Umami’s metric definitions, privacy notice, and data processing agreement.

Umami analytics are cookieless. We store your accepted or rejected choice under 2badideas-analytics-v1 in your browser’s local storage for 180 days, then ask again. If storage is unavailable, a choice may last only for the current page. Clearing this site’s browser data removes your choice along with game saves and cached files. We do not queue offline analytics or send past game activity when you later accept.

We do not use advertising pixels, session recordings, heatmaps, custom visitor IDs, or game-performance/error reporting. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Fonts and game assets are served locally; Umami is the optional external analytics service.

Browser games and device storage

Sticks in the Woods runs in your browser. Its controls, scores, and progress stay in memory and are not sent to us. It has no accounts, chat, uploads, or persistent saved progress. It caches local game files for offline play after a successful online visit. Closing or reloading the game starts a new run. You can remove cached files by clearing this site’s browser data.

Into the Deep also runs in your browser. It saves game progress in local storage on your device and caches local game files, including fonts, for offline play. Saved progress is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Sap & Survive runs in your browser. It stores your character selection, sound preference, three manual save slots, and an autosave in local storage on your device. Saves include game progress and the time saved. Autosaves happen periodically during play and when you leave or switch away from the game. Character selection, sound preference, and saved game data are not sent to us or third parties and are not synced between devices by the game. They remain until replaced or cleared. To remove them, close the game, then clear this site’s browser data. This also removes other browser games’ saves and cached files for this site.

Hosting and email

GitHub Pages hosts our site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Hosting and delivery services also process technical request information, such as requested pages, browser information, and request times, to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub's data-collection notice.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and handle requests. Our hosting, delivery, and email providers process information to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address security incidents or protect legal rights.

Purposes, location, and retention

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on consent for optional Umami analytics, legitimate interests to operate a secure website and respond to correspondence, and legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw analytics consent through “Analytics choices” and may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Contact us for information about applicable international-transfer safeguards. We keep correspondence as long as needed to handle it and meet legal obligations. Providers retain operational and security logs under their own retention practices.

Umami processes analytics on our behalf under its data processing agreement, including its international-transfer provisions. We use its free Hobby plan, which provides six months of analytics retention, unless we delete the data earlier. Operational and security logs have separate provider retention practices. Rotating visitor identifiers does not delete existing analytics records. Contact our privacy address for deletion requests. Because we do not attach names, email addresses, or custom visitor IDs to analytics, we may be unable to connect an analytics record to you.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your information, or object to its use, by emailing our privacy address. We may need information to verify and locate your request. People in the EEA or UK may also complain to their local data protection authority. We do not discriminate against people for exercising applicable privacy rights.

When your browser exposes an enabled Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we keep optional analytics off even if you previously accepted. We do not sell or share information for advertising.

Children, external services, and updates

This is a general-audience site, not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not request children's names, contact details, or registrations through the site or game. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

This notice does not cover the downloadable Stick Together game, purchases on Steam, or use of Discord. Those services may have separate privacy notices. Steam and Discord links take you to services governed by their operators' privacy policies.

We will update this notice and the date above when our practices change, including when new browser games collect different information.
